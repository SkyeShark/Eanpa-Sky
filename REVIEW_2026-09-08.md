## Performance work and measurement method

Repeated static perimeter meshes now use spatially grouped instances. The
authored geometry, material and transformations are retained. In the matched
courtyard view, 59 repeated meshes became eight draw meshes and total draw
commands fell from 181 to 130. The animated gate remains independently movable.
Tests compare world transforms and ray hits; matched images differ only at
0.164% of pixels by more than two 8-bit channel values. The off/on/off control
returned an identical final image when instancing was disabled again.
This uses [Three's instanced rendering](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/InstancedMesh.html).

The reduced-resource route uses [CDP CPU throttling](https://chromedevtools.github.io/devtools-protocol/tot/Emulation/#method-setCPUThrottlingRate)
at 4× plus a compute workload calibrated to approximately 8 ms of GPU time.
The added workload is measured with GPU timestamps during the run. A completion
fence prevents unlimited GPU queuing. These are synthetic stress tests on the
same **RTX 5090 Laptop GPU (24 GB, 150 W limit)**; they do not emulate a specific
lower-end GPU, its memory capacity, driver or architecture.

Every result includes camera, resolution, weather, quality, revision, frame-time
percentiles, CPU/GPU background activity and validity flags. Runs competing with
other applications remain labeled as contended. Early before/after CPU-throttle
runs overlapped unrelated Blender bakes and are not clean speedup measurements.

Cold initialization and quality/world rebuilds still spend substantial time
preparing shaders. Moving that work behind the loading screen fixes first-use
playback stalls; it does not eliminate the compilation cost. Draw submission
and material work remain the main concern under strong CPU throttling.

tier ordering nor a speedup over an earlier version can be inferred from these

Observed shader warmup stages took **225 seconds (Performance), 165 seconds

material-submission optimization. The CPU profile identifies draw submission,
binding updates and uniform-buffer uploads as major costs; lowering only cloud
quality does not remove those scene costs. No lower-end device target is
validated by this pass.
