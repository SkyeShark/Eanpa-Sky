## Initial findings (to validate)

The native reflection shader traces previous-frame depth. Camera-bound animated geometry is not reprojected with object motion. The local fallback probe publishes a new texture and projection box abruptly after a six-frame capture. Cloud drift multiplies changing wind by total elapsed time, so weather interpolation can shift the entire field. Low-angle cloud shadows are explicitly faded out below a solar elevation of 0.16 radians.
