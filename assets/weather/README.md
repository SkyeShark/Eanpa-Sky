# Weather fields

`cirrus_ice_trails.png` is a linear, periodic cloud-opacity field generated by
`qa/bake-cirrus.py` (seed and dimensions are recorded in its JSON manifest).
There is no source photograph or third-party texture. The model deposits fine
curved trails from irregular emitter families, then blends their fine fibers
with a diffuse veil. It is a cloud-shape authoring model, not a fluid simulation.

The sky advects the 18 km tile with its wind and samples this same field for
visible cirrus and celestial-light attenuation. Mip filtering resolves distant
fibers without adding a per-pixel procedural plume loop. A host may supply a
different linear, repeat-wrapped texture through `textures.cirrus`.

Morphology reference: the [WMO description of cirrus](https://cloudatlas.wmo.int/en/explanatory-remarks-and-special-clouds-cirrus.html)
distinguishes fine fibers, tufts, and curved or hooked trails.
